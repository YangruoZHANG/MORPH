Placement and SHA-256 for exact core and contact inputs are listed in `external_inputs.csv`. Paths are relative to the repository root. Original study URLs identify upstream datasets and do not directly supply all project-specific files listed there.

The historical supplementary exports include the approved figures and selected state and edge tables. Full redraw requires the archived plotting inputs, including per-cell background records. The candidate-contact module includes archived result comparisons; its inputs/ subdirectory is supplied separately. Project-derived inputs require a deposition or documented derivation before the manuscript's final availability statement is issued.
